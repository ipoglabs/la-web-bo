1. Register

Four ways to prove who you are, then one shared ending that creates the account. If the email/phone already has an account, the user is simply signed in and registration stops there.

3. Post an ad

Every new ad starts as pending and is invisible to buyers until staff approve it (journey 4).

StepPageWhat the seller doesWhat the system does
1Header → "Post Ad"Clicks Post AdNot signed in → /login?next=/post
2/post/select-categoryPicks category and sub-categoryOnly categories enabled for the active country are shown
3/post/detailsTitle, description, price and the category's own fieldsForm shape comes from the post-form schema for that sub-category
4/post/pick-locationPicks the locationStores address + coordinates
5/post/upload-photoUploads photosImages resized into variants
6/post/previewChecks the ad and submits—
7Server (addPost.ts)—Validates against the schema (and Singapore short-let rules), creates an adsId, saves with status: pending, logs POST_CREATED
8Server—Dropdown values the seller typed that aren't options yet → Option requests queue in the BO
9EmailReceives "Your listing is under review"The "Your listing is live" email comes only after approval (journey 4)
10BOWaitsAd appears in BO → Ads with an amber "pending" count in the nav

Ad statuses

StatusSeen by buyers?How it gets there
pendingNoNew ad, or any edit by the seller
activeYesApproved by staff; or seller resumes / renews
rejectedNoRejected by staff with a reason; seller is emailed (production only)
offNoSeller or staff paused it, the owner's account was suspended (pausedForOwnerSuspension), or an admin suspended the ad
expiredNoPast its end date; seller can renew for 60 days
closedNoSeller marked it sold / closed
deletedNoSeller deleted it, or the owner's account was deleted

Suspended isn't a status: it's a separate flag (isSuspended). A suspended ad is hidden whatever its status, and only an admin's Unsuspend brings it back (journey 14).

14. Suspension & appeal

Suspend an ad

StepWhereWhoWhat happens
1BO → ad page → SuspendAdmin, Super AdminInternal reason required. Ad hidden at once; its status is saved; seller emailed "listing removed" with a link to the support page to appeal
2BO → ad page → Unsuspend…Admin, Super AdminReason required. The ad goes back to the status it had before. If that was live, the seller gets "your listing is live" again

Suspend a user

StepWhereWhoWhat happens
1BO → user page → SuspendAdmin, Super AdminReason required. User can't sign in; all their live ads go offline (marked pausedForOwnerSuspension); their pending ads can't be approved. Emailed "account suspended" with a link to appeal
2BO → user page → UnsuspendAdmin, Super AdminReason required. Exactly the ads taken offline by the suspension go live again; ads the seller paused, or an admin suspended one by one, stay as they are. Emailed "account reinstated"

Appeal

StepWhereWhoWhat happens
1Email → support pageSellerContacts support to appeal
2BO → user or ad → Escalate to Admin…SupportChecks who they are, escalates with "Appeal:" and what they said. No promises on the outcome
3BO → EscalationsAn Admin who didn't make the original callDecides as a fresh case: unsuspend or keep. Resolves the escalation with the reasoning

15. Escalate to an Admin
StepWhereWhoWhat happens
1Ad page, user page or report → "Escalate to Admin…"Moderator, SupportWrites what the admins should look at (internal). One open escalation per ad / user / report
2Ad and user pagesAll staffAn amber banner shows the case is already with the admins
3BO → Escalations (nav badge, dashboard card)Admin, Super AdminOpen cases, oldest first. Take the action on the ad or user page as usual
4BO → Escalations → Resolve…SameWrites what was decided; the case moves to Resolved
5Audit log—Both steps logged against the ad / user / report. Resolved escalations are deleted after 2 years
