BRD · Section 1

LokalAds user journeys: business requirements

How every journey on the live site works, what the business rules are, and where the back office comes in. For Super Admins and Admins.

Document
User journeys BRD
Audience
Super Admin, Admin
Last reviewed
24 Nov 2026
Reviewed against
la-web and back-office code

1.1 Purpose

One place that states, journey by journey, what the site and the back office must do: who takes part, what starts it, the steps, the rules that apply and how it ends. Staff use it to answer user questions and to check a decision against the rules; product uses it as the baseline for changes.

1.2 Scope

  • In scope: the 15 journeys in the catalogue, covering accounts, ads, moderation, buyers, chat, reports, alerts, scheduled jobs, the activity trail, suspensions and escalations.
  • Out of scope: back-office staff management, post-form editing and dev tools, except where a journey touches them.

BRD · J01

Register

Primary actor
New user
Trigger
Opens /register
Outcome
New account, signed in
BO touchpoint
Users
Source
docs/registration-flow.html

Business objective

Let a person create an account with a proven identity (Google, Apple, email or phone) and no password, without ever creating a second account for the same email or phone.

Preconditions

  • The user isn't signed in.

Process flow

StepMethodWhereWhat the user doesWhat the system does
1All/registerPicks Google, Apple, Email or Phone—
2aGoogleGoogle consent screenApproves/api/auth/google-callback looks up the email. Match → signed in. No match → google_pending cookie, go to step 3
2bAppleApple consent → /register/apple-emailApproves, then types a real email and the 6-digit code sent to itLooks up appleEmailId. Apple often hides the email, so an extra email-code step is required
2cEmail/register/verifyEnters email, then the 6-digit codemagic-link → verify-magic → resolve-identity. Match → signed in
2dPhone/register/verifyEnters phone, then the 6-digit codeCode sent by Twilio (mocked for +91). resolve-identity on primaryNumber
3All/register/detailsFull name, date of birth, gender—
4All/register/rolePicks roles or skips—
5All—SubmitsPOST /api/auth/complete-profile: re-checks proof, duplicate check, User.create, session cookie, REGISTERED activity
6AllHomeLands signed in—

Business rules

IDRule
BR-J01.1If the email or phone already has an account, the user is signed in and registration stops there.
BR-J01.2Apple sign-ups must also verify a real email with a 6-digit code.
BR-J01.3The server re-checks the proof and duplicates when the profile is submitted, not only at the start.
BR-J01.4Name, date of birth and gender are required; roles are optional.

Outcome

A new User, a session cookie and a REGISTERED activity row (J13).

BRD · J02

Sign in & sign out

Primary actor
Returning user
Trigger
Opens /login, or a protected page
Outcome
Session cookie
BO touchpoint
Users (suspend blocks sign-in)
Source
journeys/04, code

Business objective

Let an existing user back in with the same passwordless methods they registered with, and keep suspended or deleted accounts out.

Preconditions

  • The user has an account (otherwise J01).

Process flow

StepWhereWhat happensNotes for staff
1/login (or any protected page → /login?next=…)User picks Google, Apple, email code or phone codeNo passwords for site users; codes are 6 digits
2ServerIdentity verified, account looked upSuspended or deleted accounts are refused; no session is issued
3ServercreateUserSession() sets the cookie, stamps lastLoginAt, clears dormantNudgedAt, logs LOGINFeeds the dormant-user nudge (J12)
4BrowserRedirect to ?next or home—
5Any pageSign out clears the sessionSigned-out sessions are deleted after 30 days, idle ones after 60 (data retention job)

Business rules

IDRule
BR-J02.1Site users never have passwords; codes are 6 digits.
BR-J02.2Suspended or deleted accounts get no session.
BR-J02.3Every sign-in resets the dormant-nudge clock.
BR-J02.4Signed-out sessions are deleted after 30 days, idle sessions after 60.

Outcome

Signed in and returned to the page they wanted, with a LOGIN activity row.

Post an ad

Primary actor

Outcome

BO touchpoint

Source

Business objective

Let a seller list an item with the fields its category needs, while nothing reaches buyers until staff have reviewed it.

Preconditions

  • Signed in (otherwise sent to /login?next=/post).

Process flow

StepPageWhat the seller doesWhat the system does
1Header → "Post Ad"Clicks Post AdNot signed in → /login?next=/post
2/post/select-categoryPicks category and sub-categoryOnly categories enabled for the active country are shown
3/post/detailsTitle, description, price and the category's own fieldsForm shape comes from the post-form schema for that sub-category
4/post/pick-locationPicks the locationStores address + coordinates
5/post/upload-photoUploads photosImages resized into variants
6/post/previewChecks the ad and submits—
7Server (addPost.ts)—Validates against the schema (and Singapore short-let rules), creates an adsId, saves with status: pending, logs POST_CREATED
8Server—Dropdown values the seller typed that aren't options yet → Option requests queue in the BO
9EmailReceives "Your listing is under review"The "Your listing is live" email comes only after approval (J04)
10BOWaitsAd appears in BO → Ads with an amber "pending" count in the nav

Business rules

IDRule
BR-J03.1Every new ad starts as pending and is invisible to buyers until staff approve it.
BR-J03.2Only categories enabled for the active country can be picked.
BR-J03.3The ad must pass the sub-category's post-form schema; Singapore short-lets have extra rules.
BR-J03.4Typed dropdown values that aren't options yet go to the Option requests queue.

Outcome

A pending ad in the moderation queue, an "under review" email and a POST_CREATED activity row. Statuses are listed in R1.

BRD · J04

Ad review (moderation)

Primary actor
Moderator, Admin, Super Admin
Trigger
An ad is pending
Outcome
Ad active, rejected, paused or suspended
BO touchpoint
Ads, Escalations, Audit log
Source
code (BO)

Business objective

Only ads that meet the rules go live, every decision is recorded, and serious cases reach an Admin.

Preconditions

  • The ad is pending (new or edited, J03 / J05).

Process flow

StepWhereWhoWhat happensRecorded in
1BO → Ads (?status=pending)Moderator, Admin, Super AdminOpens the queue, reviews one ad or ticks several—
2aAd page / bulk barSameApprove → active, ad goes live; seller emailed "Your listing is live" (production only). Refused if the seller's account is suspended or closedBO audit log; seller's activity (AD_APPROVED)
2bAd page / bulk barSameReject with a reason → rejected; seller emailed once per rejectionBO audit log; AD_REJECTED
2cAd pageSamePause a live ad → off; no emailBO audit log
2dAd pageAdmin, Super AdminSuspend (scam, illegal, dangerous) → hidden, status saved for later; seller emailed "listing removed" with a link to appeal (J14)BO audit log; AD_SUSPENDED
2eAd pageModeratorEscalate to Admin… instead of only rejecting (J15)BO audit log; Escalations
3Seller's /my-adsSellerSees the status and reason; can edit and resubmit (back to pending, "under review" email)POST_UPDATED

Business rules

IDRule
BR-J04.1An ad can't be approved while the seller's account is suspended or closed.
BR-J04.2Rejecting requires a reason; the seller is emailed once per rejection.
BR-J04.3Only Admins and Super Admins can suspend.
BR-J04.4A suspended ad can't be approved, paused or rejected, singly or in bulk.
BR-J04.5Moderators escalate scams, weapons, drugs, adult content, stolen or counterfeit goods and anything involving minors, rather than only rejecting.
BR-J04.6Seller emails are sent in production only.

Outcome

The ad has a decided status, the decision is in the BO audit log and on the seller's activity trail.

StepWhereWhat happens
1Ad page → "Contact seller"Buyer starts the chat

3/chat/[id]Send messages (logs MESSAGE_SENT), make offers, mark as read

Primary actor
Any signed-in user, then staff
Trigger
"Report this ad"
Outcome
Report actioned or dismissed; reporter emailed
BO touchpoint
Reports, Ads, Escalations
Source
code, journeys/07

Business objective

Users can flag bad ads, staff act on them in priority order, and repeat offenders reach an Admin.

Preconditions

  • Signed in, and no open report from this user on this ad.

Process flow

StepWhereWhoWhat happens
1Ad page → "Report this ad"Signed-in userPicks a reason
2Server—Report saved as pending; the seller sees "Under review" on /my-ads
3BO → Reports (Open tab)Moderator, Admin, Super AdminWorks the list high priority first, then oldest first. Each row shows the ad's state and the seller's count of valid reports (red at 3+)
4aBO → Reports → Review…SameMark reviewed: checked, still open
4bSameSameDismiss: the report was unfounded; ad unchanged
4cSameSame (suspend: Admin+)Actioned: pick what happens to the ad (reject with a reason for the seller, pause, or suspend). "Already handled" only if the ad isn't live. The ad's other open reports close too
5EmailReporterWhen the report closes (actioned or dismissed), the reporter gets "Resolved" or "Dismissed" (production only)
6BOModerator / SupportSeller with 3+ valid reports → escalate their account for a suspension review (J15)
7Audit log—Each decision with a required note, the staff member and the time

Business rules

IDRule
BR-J09.1One open report per user per ad.
BR-J09.2Staff work reports high priority first, then oldest first.
BR-J09.3Every decision needs a note, recorded with the staff member and time.
BR-J09.4Actioning a report closes the ad's other open reports.
BR-J09.5"Already handled" is only allowed when the ad isn't live. Suspending needs Admin or above.
BR-J09.6The reporter gets a fixed neutral line, never the staff note.
BR-J09.7A seller with 3 or more valid reports is escalated for a suspension review.
BR-J09.8Closed reports can't be reopened.

Outcome

The report is dismissed or actioned, the ad changed if needed, the reporter emailed and the decision audited.

Business objective

Users can leave and have their personal data removed, while the people they chatted with keep their history and support keeps a short window for legal requests.

An anonymised account, all ads deleted, a confirmation email, and chat history kept for the other side.

BRD · J14

Suspension & appeal

Primary actor
Admin, then the user
Trigger
BO → ad or user page → Suspend
Outcome
Ad / account restored or kept suspended
BO touchpoint
Ads, Users, Escalations
Source
code (BO)

Business objective

Take harmful ads or accounts off the site at once, and give the user a fair, independent appeal.

Process flow: suspend an ad

StepWhereWhoWhat happens
1BO → ad page → SuspendAdmin, Super AdminInternal reason required. Ad hidden at once; its status is saved; seller emailed "listing removed" with a link to the support page to appeal
2BO → ad page → Unsuspend…Admin, Super AdminReason required. The ad goes back to the status it had before. If that was live, the seller gets "your listing is live" again

Process flow: suspend a user

StepWhereWhoWhat happens
1BO → user page → SuspendAdmin, Super AdminReason required. User can't sign in; all their live ads go offline (marked pausedForOwnerSuspension); their pending ads can't be approved. Emailed "account suspended" with a link to appeal
2BO → user page → UnsuspendAdmin, Super AdminReason required. Exactly the ads taken offline by the suspension go live again; ads the seller paused, or an admin suspended one by one, stay as they are. Emailed "account reinstated"

Process flow: appeal

StepWhereWhoWhat happens
1Email → support pageSellerContacts support to appeal
2BO → user or ad → Escalate to Admin…SupportChecks who they are, escalates with "Appeal:" and what they said. No promises on the outcome
3BO → EscalationsAn Admin who didn't make the original callDecides as a fresh case: unsuspend or keep. Resolves the escalation with the reasoning

Business rules

IDRule
BR-J14.1Only Admins and Super Admins can suspend or unsuspend, and each needs a reason.
BR-J14.2Suspended isn't a status but a separate flag (isSuspended). A suspended ad is hidden whatever its status, and only Unsuspend brings it back.
BR-J14.3Unsuspending an ad restores the exact status it had before.
BR-J14.4Unsuspending a user brings back only the ads the suspension took offline.
BR-J14.5An appeal is decided by an Admin who didn't make the original call. Support makes no promises on the outcome.

Outcome

The ad or account is restored, or stays suspended, with the reasoning recorded on the escalation.

BRD · Reference R1

Ad statuses

StatusSeen by buyers?How it gets there
pendingNoNew ad, or any edit by the seller
activeYesApproved by staff; or seller resumes / renews
rejectedNoRejected by staff with a reason; seller is emailed (production only)
offNoSeller or staff paused it, the owner's account was suspended (pausedForOwnerSuspension), or an admin suspended the ad
expiredNoPast its end date; seller can renew for 60 days
closedNoSeller marked it sold / closed
deletedNoSeller deleted it, or the owner's account was deleted

Suspended isn't a status: it's a separate flag (isSuspended). A suspended ad is hidden whatever its status, and only an admin's Unsuspend brings it back (J14).
